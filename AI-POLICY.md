# AI Usage Policy

OpenSC2K embraces AI-assisted development as part of its development workflow. Large language models and other AI tools may be used for code generation, refactoring, debugging, research, documentation, testing, and other development tasks.

AI is treated as a development tool, not as a substitute for human understanding, judgment, or responsibility.

## Human Responsibility

All code and other contributions must have a human responsible for them.

Anyone contributing AI-assisted work should understand the changes they are submitting and be able to explain what the code does, why the change was made, and how it interacts with the surrounding system without relying on an AI tool to do so.

AI-generated output should not be accepted simply because it compiles, passes tests, or appears correct. Contributors are responsible for reviewing, testing, and validating their work before submitting it.

## Disclosure

AI-assisted contributions are welcome.

When AI has been substantially involved in a contribution, contributors should disclose that assistance. This can be as simple as mentioning the tool used and the general extent of its involvement in the pull request description.

For example:

> AI-assisted using Claude Code for implementation and refactoring. Changes were reviewed and tested by the contributor.

Minor uses such as autocomplete, grammar correction, formatting, or asking an AI tool a general programming question do not require disclosure.

The goal of disclosure is transparency, not discouragement of AI-assisted development.

## Issues, Discussions, and Documentation

AI tools may be used when preparing issues, discussions, documentation, and other project communication.

However, anything submitted to the project should be reviewed by a human first. AI-generated content should be edited as necessary to ensure that it is accurate, relevant, and concise.

Please avoid submitting large amounts of unreviewed AI-generated text. Other people have to spend their time reading and evaluating what is submitted.

## Maintainer Use of AI

OpenSC2K itself is developed with substantial assistance from AI tools, including large language models.

Maintainers may use AI tools at their discretion for implementation, refactoring, debugging, testing, research, documentation, code review, and other development activities.

Regardless of how a change is produced, maintainers remain responsible for the architecture, design decisions, review, testing, and final implementation included in the project.

## There Are Humans Here

Every issue, discussion, pull request, and contribution ultimately involves another person spending their time reading, reviewing, testing, or responding to it.

AI makes it possible to produce code and text extremely quickly, but it does not make reviewing that work free.

Please respect the time of maintainers and contributors by understanding, reviewing, and refining AI-assisted work before submitting it.

Low-effort or unreviewed AI-generated contributions may be closed or rejected.

## AI Is Welcome Here

OpenSC2K is not an anti-AI project.

AI tools are actively used in the development of OpenSC2K and are considered useful development tools when used responsibly.

This policy exists because the quality of AI-assisted work depends heavily on the person directing, reviewing, and validating it. The person submitting a contribution—not the AI tool—is ultimately responsible for that contribution.

Good contributions are welcome regardless of whether AI helped create them.
